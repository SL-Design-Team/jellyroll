trigger
Field with calendar icon trailing. Displays selected date(s) formatted per locale.
popover
Floats below the trigger. Single picker = one month. Range picker = two months side-by-side + preset sidebar.
presets
Range picker offers common selections (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This/Last month, Custom). Active preset highlighted Blue-100.
states
Today: 1.5px Blue-600 border · Selected: blue fill · Range: blue tint between endpoints, solid blue endpoints · Muted: previous/next month, still live · Inert: previous/next month in the two-month view, not live · Disabled: grey, no hover.
grid
Always six rows, padded with out-of-month days. The grid height must not change as you page through months — a popover that resizes under the cursor moves the day you were aiming at. A trailing row that is entirely out-of-month is expected; leave it.
overflow
Depends on the view, and the two rules are opposites on purpose.
Single month — out-of-month days are muted but live: they hover, select, and take the range tint, and an endpoint landing on one renders as a full pill. This is the only copy of that date on screen.
Two months — every out-of-month day is inert: muted, unhoverable, unfocusable (tabindex="-1" aria-hidden="true"), never tinted, never an endpoint cap. The neighbouring pane already shows those dates live, and a date must be live in exactly one pane. Tint both copies and a boundary-crossing range draws twice, side by side, reading as two ranges.
crossing
A range that spans the two panes ends flat at the last day of the left pane and resumes flat at the first day of the right. Endpoint pills mark the true start and end only — a pill means "the range stops here," so a cap at a pane edge is a lie. The May pane above closes square on 31; the June pane opens square on 1.
validation
Error borders Red-600 with a message below. Success is border-only (or omitted) — the trailing calendar icon holds the slot, so there's no room for a success check or message; the field exposes no success message.
keyboard
Arrow keys move day-by-day · Home/End to the ends of the week · PgUp/PgDn month-by-month · Shift+PgUp/PgDn year · Enter selects · Esc closes the popover, or abandons a half-picked range. One roving tab stop per grid: Tab reaches the focused day, arrows move within.
demo
Both pickers above are live — click, hover and type in them. The range grid previews the pending range as you hover the second endpoint, and Apply / Cancel commit or revert against the trigger. The JS is progressive enhancement over the static grids, which remain the markup reference if it doesn't run; it also holds the overflow rule in executable form, so read paintCell rather than reimplementing from prose.
a11y
role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-label="Choose date". Grid: role="grid"; day buttons: role="gridcell" aria-selected aria-current="date".
